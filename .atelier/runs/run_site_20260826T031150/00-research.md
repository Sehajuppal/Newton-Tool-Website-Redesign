# Research Digest: Newton Tool Repair Ltd.

## Executive Summary
Newton Tool Repair Ltd. is an established industrial equipment and power tool repair facility based in Surrey, BC (#114 - 7533 135th Street) [1]. They serve trade professionals, general contractors, framing teams, woodworkers, and industrial crews across Metro Vancouver [1][5]. They are a certified, factory-authorized warranty and service center for premier global manufacturers including DeWalt, Metabo HPT, Rolair Systems, Porter-Cable, Paslode, and Flex Power Tools [2][4].

Beyond electrical and pneumatic power tool diagnostics and overhauls, Newton Tool is renowned in the regional trade community for precision blade sharpening services (including custom carbide saws, band saws, and dado sets) [3].
