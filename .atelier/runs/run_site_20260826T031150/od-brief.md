## Audience & Primary Outcome
- **Audience:** General contractors, framing crews, finishing carpenters, cabinet makers, roofers, landscapers, and industrial fabricators across Metro Vancouver (Surrey, Delta, Langley, Burnaby, Richmond) who need zero downtime on their equipment.
- **Primary Outcome:** Drive phone calls, drop-off service visits, precision blade sharpening drop-ins, and interactive repair quote submissions.

## Design Direction & Aesthetic
- **Style:** `industrial-precision` — Heavy-duty workshop strength fused with Swiss technical clarity and precision machine tool geometry. Deep graphite cast iron grounds, machined tool steel cards, crisp high-contrast white typography, brushed steel specs, and high-visibility industrial safety amber/tool gold accents.
- **Dials:** Variance: 6, Motion: 5, Density: 8, Graphics: 6.
- **Typography:** Space Grotesk (Display / Headers), Plus Jakarta Sans (Body), JetBrains Mono (Technical callouts, part specs, diagnostics, model numbers).
- **Palette Intent:**
  - Ground: Deep industrial charcoal slate (`#121316` / `#0f1013`)
  - Raised Cards: Machined tool steel dark surface (`#1a1d24` / `#222631`)
  - Ink: High-contrast optical white (`#f4f4f6`)
  - Muted Ink: Brushed aluminum grey (`#94a3b8`)
  - Border: Precision anodized hairline stroke (`#2a2e3b`)
  - Accent: Calibrated industrial safety amber gold (`#f59e0b` / `#fbbf24`)

## Anti-AI-Slop & Strict Refusal Rules
- NO generic SaaS purple, violet, indigo, or neon cyan glow gradients.
- NO floating abstract 3D spheres or glassmorphic blur blobs.
- NO cartoon wrenches, robot mechanics, or generic clip art.
- NO fake invented metrics ("99.999% uptime for your drill"). Keep copy honest, grounded, and trade-focused.
- NO microscopic unreadable grey text on dark backgrounds.
- Single page or full multi-section rich interactive landing page with responsive layouts, crisp SVG icons, and clean structure.
